About Udaan AI

"From immediate needs to dignified, sustained livelihoods."

Udaan AI is an open, barrier-aware livelihood pathways platform designed for adults experiencing homelessness, long-term unemployment, displacement, or acute economic insecurity in Andhra Pradesh, India.

The Barrier-Aware Philosophy

Traditional employment boards operate on the assumption of baseline stability: that an applicant possesses a stable address, government ID documentation, regular meals, transportation money, and active bank accounts. For vulnerable adults, these assumed prerequisites form an insurmountable wall.

Udaan AI breaks this cycle by treating practical barriers as primary variables. When a user requests work assistance, our algorithm:

  • Detects immediate acute needs (night shelter, hot food, basic medical safety) and prioritizes verified community relief.
  • Identifies prerequisite hurdles (lack of Aadhaar copy, absence of Jan Dhan account, transport distance) and links them to specialized civic desks.
  • Recommends verified, fair-wage livelihood openings and free vocational training without upfront tuition fees.
  • Constructs a personal 6-step sequential roadmap where each step clearly indicates its purpose, verified contact details, and progress milestones.

Ethical AI Policies

Transparent AI Assitance We use Gemini AI solely to understand natural language expressions and extract skills without judgment. AI never decides eligibility, invents vacancies, or replaces human review.
No Sensitive Inferences The system strictly prohibits inferring or recording caste, religion, criminal record, or sensitive personal histories. Dignity is non-negotiable.

Andhra Pradesh Pilot Initiative

Our pilot is focused on the urban corridors of Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, and Tirupati, collaborating with municipal welfare desks, registered NGOs, and fair employers committed to transparent compensation.
