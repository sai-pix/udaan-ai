Privacy-First Data Architecture

Dignity, informed consent, and minimal data footprint.

Strict Zero-Forced-KYC Principle

Udaan AI never demands Aadhaar numbers, biometric scans, legal identity documents, home addresses, photographs, or personal life histories as a prerequisite to access pathways.

1. Data Minimization

We collect only information voluntarily provided to generate actionable livelihood pathways: preferred language, skills, broad locality/city, practical barriers, and requested support categories.

2. Explicit Consent-Based Sharing

Your profile is never publicly browsable. Employers and NGOs cannot search a list of vulnerable individuals. Information is shared only when you explicitly trigger a referral request to a specific chosen partner organization.

3. Right to Erasure (Delete My Data)

You hold complete ownership of your session profile. You can permanently erase your profile and generated pathways from our active database at any time with a single click.

4. Non-Discrimination & Algorithmic Boundaries

Our rule-based matching engine operates entirely independently from large language models. The system never infers or records caste, religion, disability, gender identity, or criminal history.
